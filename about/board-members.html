Board of Directors

Home / About Us / Board of Directors

Our Board comprises distinguished professionals with extensive experience in banking, finance, law, and corporate governance, providing strategic guidance and oversight to CFMARC's operations.

Kishor Kharat

Chairman (Independent Director)

Mr. Kishor Kharat is an acclaimed Banker with 41 years of rich banking experience, out of which more than 6 years as an MD & CEO of Indian Bank, IDBI Bank & Bank of Baroda. He was Board Member of total 14 organizations which includes Union Bank of India, EXIM Bank of India IDBI Asset Management Limited etc. Also, he has 7 years banking experiences in foreign countries (i.e. United Arab Emirates (UAE) and Trinidad & Tobago, West Indies).

Om Prakash Porwal

Director

Mr. Porwal has over 30 years of professional experience in the Financial Sector. He has fortified his skills on financial structuring and stress resolution. He is the Promoter and Director of Chartered Finance Management Limited (CFML), a company engaged in providing advisory services in Banking and Finance, in India and overseas.

Susobhan Sinha

Director (Independent)

Susobhan Singha is a retired as Regional Director, Reserve Bank of India, Kolkata. He is an acclaimed Banker with 33+ years of rich banking experience. A Central Banker by profession, Susobhan in his career spanning more than three decades, held various top management positions including Secretary to the Central Board of RBI between 2017 and 2020 which provided him comprehensive experience of the country’s economy and the financial sector as also functioning of a high-powered Board and valuable lessons in strategic decision making and governance.

Mukesh Kumar Gupta

Director (Independent)

Superannuated as the Managing Director of Life Insurance Corporation of India – a 36 trillion Rupee company with 290 million individual policyholders. Has the distinction of working for over 37 years and in all major streams that included Marketing, Personnel, Investments, to name a few. Strong ability to solve complex company problems using excellent judgement and decision-making skills. Proven ability of developing strong management teams for maximizing profitability and efficiency. Track record of developing business verticals into dynamic and progressive enterprises.

Santanu Sen

Managing Director & CEO

Mr. Sen has wide experience in stressed asset resolution and corporate credit. He was Senior Principal - Value enhancement group at Piramal Capital and Housing Finance. He has also been associated with IL&FS group and been responsible for resolutions, investments, strategic initiatives and asset management. His rich experience and dynamism will surely lead the growth of CFMARC in the coming months.
