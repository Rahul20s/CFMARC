  • Risk transfer solutions

Borrower Resolution Support

CFM ARC believes in working constructively with borrowers to achieve practical and mutually beneficial resolution outcomes.

Borrowers whose loan accounts have been assigned to CFM ARC are encouraged to engage with our team to explore resolution options.

Possible resolution mechanisms may include:

One Time Settlement (OTS)

Structured repayment plans

Asset monetization strategies

Business restructuring solutions

Loan Settlement and Resolution

Borrowers may approach CFM ARC to explore settlement opportunities for resolving their outstanding dues.

The settlement process typically involves:

Submission of settlement request

Borrowers submit their settlement proposal with supporting documentation

Evaluation of borrower financial position

Comprehensive assessment of financial capacity and asset valuation

Proposal for resolution

Detailed settlement terms and repayment structure presented

Final settlement agreement

Mutual agreement on terms and legal documentation

Closure of account upon fulfillment

Final closure after complete settlement of agreed terms

Borrowers interested in exploring settlement options may submit a request through the website or contact our resolution team.

Our Resolution Approach

CFM ARC adopts a structured and transparent approach to the resolution of stressed assets.

Acquisition

Financial assets are acquired from banks and financial institutions through legally compliant assignment processes.

Assessment

Each asset is evaluated to determine the most appropriate resolution strategy.

Engagement

CFM ARC engages with borrowers, investors, and other stakeholders to explore viable resolution options.

Resolution

Resolution strategies may include settlement arrangements, restructuring plans, asset sales, or legal recovery where necessary.
